    Brooklyn dance music with roots.

    Dandelion Musica was founded in 2024 by DJ and producer Danny Deleon. The label puts out Soulful, Afro, Deep, Latin, Tech House, Deep Tech, Techno, Disco, Funky House and Indie Dance built for real dance floors: warm, percussive, made to move people.

    Its sister imprint DeLiOn MusiK carries open-format releases beyond house.
