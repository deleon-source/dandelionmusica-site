Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Dandelion Musica is a record label run by Danny Deleon LLC in Brooklyn, New York. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email and name, when you join our list, download a track through one of our links, or send us a demo.
  • How you interact with our emails: whether an email was opened and which links were clicked.
  • How links we send are used: when release pages, private streams or files we share with artists, DJs and partners are opened or downloaded, from what type of device (phone or computer), and when.
  • Website visits: this site uses Meta's pixel and embedded Spotify players, which may set cookies to measure visits and ads.

Why

To send you new music and label news you signed up for, to get promos and files to the right people, and to understand what is working so we can plan and improve future releases. This measurement is for the label's own planning.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a few service providers to run the label, such as our email platform (Kit), file storage (Google), our website and database hosts, and Meta for ads. They process data for us under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are on our list or we work together, and for a reasonable time after for our records.

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing bookings@dandelionmusica.com.
  • If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including to object to this measurement. Our basis for it is our legitimate interest in running and improving the label.
